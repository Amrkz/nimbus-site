A sandboxed download manager for Mac. Download HTTP, HTTPS, and FTP files, pause and resume, run queues, and send links from Safari or the Share sheet.

Nimbus requires macOS 13 or later. It is free to use. Optional Nimbus Pro adds a scheduler, overnight queues, and per-queue limits, as a Lifetime one-time purchase or a Yearly subscription.
