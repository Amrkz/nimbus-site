Nimbus

A sandboxed download manager for Mac. Accelerate HTTP and HTTPS files, pause and resume, run queues on a schedule, and send links from Safari or the Share sheet.

Nimbus requires macOS 13 or later. This App Store edition downloads direct HTTP and HTTPS files only — not YouTube or other media sites, not entire websites, and not torrents.
