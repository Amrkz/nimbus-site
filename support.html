Frequently asked questions

How do I add a link?

Open Nimbus and paste an HTTP, HTTPS, or FTP link, or drop a list of links onto the window. You can also choose File → Add Download…. Pick a destination, then start the transfer.

When a server supports HTTP Range requests, Nimbus can split a file across several connections to finish sooner. If the server does not support ranges, Nimbus uses a single connection.

How do I use the Safari extension?

Turn the Nimbus extension on in Safari Settings, under Extensions. It sends a link you chose to Nimbus on the same Mac.

How do I use the Share extension?

In Safari or another app, open the Share sheet and choose Nimbus. The link is sent to Nimbus on the same Mac.

How do I pause and resume a download?

Pause a transfer in the Nimbus window, from the menu bar icon, or with Downloads → Pause. You can resume it later, including after you quit Nimbus or restart your Mac, as long as the transfer was already started.

Where do files go?

You choose the destination: your Downloads folder, or another folder you pick. If Nimbus cannot save outside Downloads, choose the folder again. Access to other folders is granted only when you select them.

What does Nimbus Pro include?

Nimbus is free to use. Accelerated downloads, pause and resume, queues, the Safari extension, and the Share menu stay free. Optional Nimbus Pro adds:

  • Scheduler: start and stop download queues at set times and days
  • Overnight queues: run big downloads while you sleep, then quit Nimbus when done
  • Per-queue limits on how many files download at once

Nimbus Pro is sold two ways. Either one unlocks Pro:

  • Lifetime — a $24.99 one-time purchase
  • Yearly — a $9.99 auto-renewing subscription

Prices are shown in the app by the App Store and may vary by region or currency.

How do I restore a purchase?

Choose Nimbus → Nimbus Pro… in the menu bar, then choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Nimbus has no separate account.

How do I cancel the Yearly subscription?

The Yearly plan renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account subscription settings. A Lifetime purchase does not renew and does not need to be cancelled.

The purchase didn’t complete.

Check your internet connection and that you’re signed in to the App Store, then try again. If you were charged but Pro is still locked, use Restore Purchases.

Is my data collected?

No. Nimbus does not collect personal data, and it does not create an account or use analytics. Downloads stay on your Mac. Purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. Read the Privacy Policy.

Nimbus Pro payments

Payment is charged to your Apple Account. The Yearly subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings. Use Restore Purchases in the app to restore a Lifetime purchase or an active Yearly subscription on another Mac signed in with the same Apple Account.
